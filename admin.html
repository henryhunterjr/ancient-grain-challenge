The drawings

Friday draw: everyone who has finished at least one lesson and hasn't already won a 5 lb bag is in the pool. Draw four each Friday, October 16 through November 27. December 1: use the final 5 lb bag option twice, then the grand prize option. Both grain options require all seven lessons. Then use the mixer option once: every registered baker has one entry plus their verified points, with no lesson required. Winning grain does not affect mixer eligibility. A baker can win one 5 lb bag across all dates; earlier 5 lb winners remain eligible for the grand prize. Explicitly flagged test entries and removed entries are excluded from every pool. For grain, each verified point is one entry. Every draw is saved and can't be undone. Winners show on the public page right away, first name and last initial only.
