How it works

  1. Register today. Learn at your own pace. You don’t need to complete a lesson or quiz to sign up. It’s free. Tell us where you’re joining from and pick the grain you’d want to win. Registering also enters you automatically in the drawing for a Bosch Universal Plus stand mixer from NutriMill.
  2. Start with one lesson, then finish all seven. Watch, pass the quiz at 70% or better, leave your YouTube comment, and mark it done. One completed lesson qualifies you for the weekly Friday draws. All seven qualify you for the final two 5 lb bags and the 25 lb grand prize on December 1.
  3. Earn bonus points. Bake with ancient grains, mill your own flour, join a Saturday Bake Along. Every verified point is one more entry in the drawing.

Earn points for learning

Earn 20 points for each completed challenge lesson. Each of the seven lessons counts once.

  1. Watch the lesson, including both videos for the Ancient Grain Sourdough Starter lesson.
  2. Take its quiz and score 70% or higher.
  3. Leave a comment on that lesson's YouTube video. Share what you learned or ask a baking question.
  4. Record your quiz score and YouTube name on the lesson page, then check that your completion appears in My entry.

Repeated comments don't earn extra points. The two optional bonus quizzes earn 20 points each, once, after you save a passing score and confirm your YouTube comment.

Official rules

Who can enter
  • You must be 18 or older. For weekly Friday draws, complete at least one Ancient Grain lesson. For the final two 5 lb bags and the 25 lb grand prize on December 1, complete all seven. For the stand mixer drawing, registering is enough. Completing a lesson means watching the video, passing the quiz at 70% or better, leaving your YouTube comment, and recording your YouTube name and quiz percentage.
  • Open to residents of the United States and Canada. Void where prohibited.
  • One registration per person. Duplicate registrations will be removed.
  • Henry Hunter's household and employees of Grand Teton Ancient Grains and NutriMill aren't eligible.
Dates
  • The challenge opens October 5, 2026.
  • Friday drawings for 5 lb bags: October 16, 23, 30 and November 6, 13, 20, 27.
  • Registration and task check-offs close Sunday, November 29, 2026 at 11:59 pm Eastern.
  • The grand prize, the last two 5 lb bags and the Bosch Universal Plus stand mixer are drawn on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, live in the Crust & Crumb Academy.
How to qualify and how entries work
  • For weekly Friday draws, complete at least one lesson before that drawing. For the final two 5 lb bags and the 25 lb grand prize on December 1, complete all seven lessons by Sunday, November 29, 2026 at 11:59 pm Eastern.
  • Every verified point is one entry. More points mean better odds, but any qualified baker can win.
  • Mixer drawing: every registered baker gets one entry automatically, with no lesson required. Each verified point adds one more entry.
  • To mark a lesson done, you leave a comment on that lesson's YouTube video and tell us the YouTube name you commented under, plus your quiz score. We check the comments for the bakers who get drawn. We also check the links you send for bonus tasks. A check-off we can't confirm doesn't count.
  • Claiming tasks you didn't do will get you removed from the challenge.
Prizes and the drawing
  • Grand prize: one 25 lb bag of grain from Grand Teton Ancient Grains, shipped to the winner. Drawn December 1 from every baker who finished all seven lessons.
  • Stand mixer: one Bosch Universal Plus stand mixer, provided by NutriMill and shipped to the winner. Drawn December 1 from every registered baker who hasn't been disqualified. Winning grain doesn't stop you from winning the mixer.
  • 5 lb prizes: thirty bags of grain from Grand Teton Ancient Grains, 150 lb in all. Four are drawn each Friday from October 16 through November 27, from registered bakers who have finished at least one lesson. The final two are drawn December 1 from bakers who have finished all seven lessons and have not already won a 5 lb bag.
  • More verified points mean more entries in every drawing. You can win one 5 lb bag at most. Winning a 5 lb bag keeps you in the running for the grand prize.
  • Winners are announced in the Academy and on this page, and contacted by email. If we don't hear back within 7 days, we draw a new winner.
  • Canadian winners must correctly answer a skill-testing question before receiving a prize.
  • Prizes can't be exchanged for cash.
